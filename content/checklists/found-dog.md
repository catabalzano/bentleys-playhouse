groups:
  - title: Right Away
    items:
      - I'm safe, and the dog is safe or I've called Animal Services (311 / 305-468-5900)
      - Checked the collar for a name tag, rabies tag or county license tag
      - Wrote down exact location, date, time and direction the dog was heading
      - Took clear photos from a few angles
  - title: Within 72 Hours (Required in Miami-Dade)
    items:
      - Microchip scan at a vet clinic or shelter
      - If chipped, looked up the number on the AAHA lookup and called the registry (contact owner within 24 hours)
      - Filed a found report with Miami-Dade Animal Services' online Lost and Found Pet Form
  - title: Searching for the Family
    items:
      - Posted the dog as found on the Fénix Animal Project Lost & Found (fenixanimalproject.org)
      - Posted on Petco Love Lost
      - Posted in local lost-and-found groups and Nextdoor
      - Put up flyers within about a mile of where the dog was found
      - Held back one identifying detail to check callers
      - Checked lost-dog posts nearby, every day
  - title: Caring for the Dog
    items:
      - Quiet room, water and bed, away from my own pets
      - Vet visit booked or done
      - Secure leash and well-fitted collar or harness for walks
