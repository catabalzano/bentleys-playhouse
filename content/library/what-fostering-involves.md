Fostering means caring for a dog in your home until they're adopted. It gives a dog quiet and individual attention that a busy shelter can't, and it teaches everyone what the dog is really like.

## How Long It Lasts

Anywhere from a few days to a few months, depending on the dog and the program. Puppies usually move on quickly. Dogs recovering from illness, injury or a hard past may stay longer.

## What Fosters Usually Do

- Daily care: food, walks, play and a safe place to sleep.
- Medications and vet or adoption appointments, if needed.
- Basic training and social time.
- Sharing updates and photos that help the dog get adopted.

## What Organizations Often Cover

Many cover vet care and medications, and some provide food and supplies. Every program is different, so ask.

## Questions to Ask First

- What supplies and vet care do you provide, and which vet do I use?
- What training or support do you offer fosters?
- How do I reach someone if there's a problem?
- Do my own pets need to be vaccinated or kept apart?
- Is there a foster-to-adopt option?
- Are there short-term options, like weekend or sleepover fostering?
