These organizations are not affiliated with Bentley's Playhouse. Prices and eligibility change, so check their pages before you go.

## Vaccines and Microchips

- **[Miami-Dade Animal Services](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser1599264624594140)** runs a walk-in vaccine clinic at the Doral shelter, with lower prices for people on public benefits.
- **[Humane Society of Greater Miami](https://www.humanesocietymiami.org/vaccine-and-preventative-care/)** offers vaccines, exams and preventives (not treatment for sick or injured animals). Call 305-696-0800.

## Spay and Neuter

- **[MDAS spay/neuter](https://www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser1596212865055119)** for Miami-Dade residents, at clinics in Doral and Homestead. Free for veterans with a DD-214.
- **[ASPCA Community Veterinary Clinic, Liberty City](https://clinic.aspca.org/clinics/miami/liberty-city)** offers lower-cost spay/neuter, exams, vaccines and microchips for Miami-Dade residents. Call 844-692-7722.

## Help Paying for Urgent Care

- **[RedRover Relief](https://redrover.org/relief/)** gives grants toward urgent, life-threatening care, including for Good Samaritans and rescuers.
- **[Pet Help Finder](https://www.pethelpfinder.org/)** searches local help by ZIP code.
- **[Humane World for Animals](https://www.humaneworld.org/en/resources/having-trouble-affording-your-pet)** keeps a list of national aid groups and payment-plan options.

## Talking to Your Vet About Cost

It's OK to ask for an estimate, to ask which treatments are most urgent and whether a payment plan is possible. Vets would rather know up front.
