## Miami-Dade

| Who | Number | For |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency | 911 | Cruelty happening now, or danger to people |
| Miami-Dade Animal Services | 311 or 305-468-5900 | Strays, injured dogs, lost and found, cruelty reports |
| Florida Highway Patrol | *347 (mobile) | A dog on an expressway |
| Miami-Dade Schools Police | 305-995-2677 | A loose dog near a school |
| State Attorney Animal Cruelty Unit | 305-547-0520 | Cruelty cases |
