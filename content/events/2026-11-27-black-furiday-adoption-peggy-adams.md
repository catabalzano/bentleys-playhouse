Peggy Adams waives adoption fees on all dogs, puppies, cats and kittens for the day after Thanksgiving, with more than 100 pets looking for homes. No appointment is needed. Palm Beach County residents pay a $20 rabies tag fee, and adoptable pets can be previewed at peggyadams.org/adopt.
