Pets can pose with Santa or in a Hanukkah scene for a $20 donation, which covers digital photos, one 4x6 print and a pet treat. Pre-registration for a time slot is required, and dogs must be on a collar and non-retractable leash. Shy or reactive pets that are friendly to people can come during an early window from 8:30 to 9 a.m.
