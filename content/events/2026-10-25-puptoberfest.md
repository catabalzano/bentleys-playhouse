Miami-Dade Animal Services' fall adoption festival with hundreds of shelter pets looking for homes. Adoption fees are waived for pets 4 months and older, and every pet goes home spayed or neutered, vaccinated and microchipped. There's also a farmers market, food trucks and live entertainment.
