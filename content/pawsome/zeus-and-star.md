Meet **Zeus and Star**, two Samoyeds who share a home. Their family reached out to us because they're struggling financially and need to find them new homes. This is a surrender case, so we're helping make sure they go somewhere safe and loving.

- **Zeus** is a 4-year-old male.
- **Star** is a 7-year-old female.

**They are mother and son** If you'd love both, wonderful! If you have room for one, that's OK too, but not ideal as they are probably bonded. Just tell us which pup you're interested in.

**No dog is adopted out unless they are spayed or neutered.** Star is already spayed, and Zeus will be neutered before he goes to his new home. This protects them from ever being used for backyard breeding. [Here's why it matters](/resources/rehoming-a-dog-safely/).
